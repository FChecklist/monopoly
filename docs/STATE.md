## Built
- Engine: rewards (all caps and the season budget inside guarded SQL), period targets with tiers and streaks, team split with per-period membership snapshots, reversals with notice and appeal, corrections (pending, max 50%), dice mode (funded bonus range), penalties (points or steps only; credit loss only if the provider can take back and the company switches it on).
- Board: our own 24-square hexagonal track; desks, upgrades, collaboration fees from a company pool, Catch-up Lane, fresh start.
- Intake: manual, CSV, signed API. Approvals, duplicate checks, audit chain.
- One credit-provider adapter (src/provider). Moneypenny today: plain credits only in v1 (hold, debit, reverse and the shop spend path are not enabled). Demo provider is dev-only (migrations-dev/), refused for a live company.
- Currency: default name plain `Moneypenny`, original coin icon, text label `moneypenny`, real price always beside it; per-tenant settings; `terms_url` link.
- AI-ready API `/api/v1`: key classes read, event_write, manager, admin, ai_read; ai_read can read and PROPOSE only; proposals are ask-only (a human admin accepts; only event.submit runs, through the normal pipeline); idempotency on every write; rate limits (30/min AI, 120 others); audit with key id and caller type; untrusted-text meta and header; tenant kill switch (default OFF), AI_KILL, Hub status.json switch; no secrets in answers. Contract: CONTRACT/openapi-v1.json (generated by scripts/make-openapi.js; a test keeps it in step) and CONTRACT/ai-api-v1.md.
- Docs: CONTRACT/, docs/DEPLOY.md, docs/ROLLBACK.md, docs/ROTATION.md, docs/REGISTRY-NOTE.md, docs/audit/round1-findings.md.

ROUND 22: (1) ONE predicate decides both what blocks the settle and what is listed (GROUP_BLOCKER in src/rewards.js): a period group with a failed row, a partly granted row, or a row the system voided (no_season, season_closed, season_cap) that no later live row replaces is listed for as long as something is owed (the settle writes the owed figure on the row; entitled <= paid unlists it), whether or not other rows cover the amount. (2) a re-issue chain (<root key>:re1, :re2, ...) is never "covered" by its own earlier rows (the closed_partial sibling, voided re-issues); a partial re-issue at the edge of the season budget counts what the close frees; the close and the insert are both guarded on the person not being erased and the event not being reversed (409 event_closed otherwise). (3) the needs-admin lists have no limit and the overview number is the real total; voided EVENT rewards of the last 30 days are listed read only; rewards_voided_silent counts only the last 30 days and only what is not listed. (4) erase also closes a partly granted row (reason voided_erased, amounts and state kept, maybe_landed marker kept) and every voided_erased path scrubs the note (one expression, SCRUB_NOTE_SQL). (5) futureOn also requires the demo provider (stub): a non-live Moneypenny company with FUTURE_OPS=1 cannot reach the shop or buying. (6) alerts: open_failed_periods and unconfirmed_credits (check the ledger; if the credit landed, record it by hand outside the app; re-issue only if it did not land). No new admin action was added.

KNOWN LIMITS (v1, not fixed on purpose): a period group (tier, streak, team) with a failed, partly granted or voided row is never paid again by the app: the admin checks the Moneypenny ledger and fixes it by hand outside the app (v2: a remedy for periods). A voided event reward (no season, season closed, cap) is listed (read only) for 30 days and is not re-issuable; grant it by hand if it is still owed. The owed figure of a listed group is refreshed when the settle runs for that group (a group no one settles stays listed with its last figure). The audit chain keeps no free text (the ledger note lives on the row only). A reward the provider answered 'duplicate_ref' for without a way to confirm it is failed (duplicate_unconfirmed) and waits for the admin.

ROUND 21 SIMPLIFICATION (BOSS): v1 keeps ONE remedy: an admin re-issues a failed or partly granted EVENT reward as a NEW grant, after checking the Moneypenny ledger. A period row answers 409 not_supported_in_v1 to a re-issue; such a group is fixed by hand outside the app. The clock still never pays a group that has a failed row (no double pay). Where it shows: the overview counter needs_admin and the admin page list 'Rewards that need an admin' (GET /api/admin/needs-admin), derived from the rows by query, no extra table: failed or partly granted event rewards (with a Re-issue button: confirm dialog and a note box), and read-only period groups with a failed row, a partly granted row or a row the system voided (no_season, season_closed, season_cap) that no other row of the group covers. Re-issue details: it is ONE batch (close of the old row and insert of the new one; the close carries the same budget guards as the insert, so the old row is never closed without the new one); its audit line is that batch's own reward.create with the admin as actor and reissue_of; asked again after it worked it answers 200 replay:true for the same amount, 409 changed for another; a new amount above 500 is refused; the note must be a string of at least 10 characters. Erasing a person scrubs the account and any admin note from the notes of their reward rows (amounts, ids, states and a maybe_landed marker stay; the reseal has nothing to bring back). ENVIRONMENT=production is set in wrangler.toml [vars] and forwarded by functions/[[path]].js, so the FUTURE_OPS guard can see it.

ROUND 20 (kept): the key of a re-issue comes from the OLD row alone (<old key>:re<N>); a re-issue is refused up front with 409 reissue_not_payable when it could not be queued; a ledger note of at least 10 characters is required; a dice season whose top bonus would exceed 500 is refused when created or published; failed and never-released waiting rows of an erased person become voided_erased and grant() never promises anything to an erased person; BUYING CREDITS (POST /api/admin/buy, startPurchase) is NOT enabled in v1 (BOSS decision, a v2 item; 409 not_enabled_in_v1, no provider call, the Buy panel says it is not available, overview buy = 'not enabled in v1'). The webhook intake for purchase.paid and similar events only records what the provider sends; nothing else in the AI API, CSV intake or the tick starts a purchase or a spend.

ROUND 19: (1) re-issue is ONE batch (old row closed + new reward inserted, guarded), an old row is never closed without the new one; (2) the period settle never pays a group that has a failed row or a re-issue chain (':re<N>' key, last_error reissued / closed_partial): only the admin's re-issue pays it; re-issue is refused when other live rows already cover it; (3) a dice bonus above 500 is refused when configured and clamped at event time; (4) open_failed_rewards honours the live flag; (5) POST /api/admin/mark-received (confirm + ledger note) closes a failed row that really landed; (6) the reissue audit line carries old id, new id and key; the answer shows the real state; (7) history is read in pages of 100 following next_before_seq before a duplicate_ref is called unconfirmed; the first-try account is sealed in the same update that counts the attempt; (8) THE SHOP IS NOT ENABLED IN v1 (BOSS decision): reserve/hold + capture + release, placeOrder/decideOrder/finishOrders, POST /api/shop/order and the item list are gated like hold/debit/reverse (src/v1.js shopOn/assertShop; spend reported false by the provider wrapper; answers 409 not_enabled_in_v1; the shop page says it is not available; overview shop = 'not enabled in v1'). It is a v2 item: the code and its tests stay, reachable only for the demo provider on a non-live company with the test-only FUTURE_OPS switch.

ROUND 18 SCOPE CUT (v1): (1) a single credit above Moneypenny's 500 maximum is refused when configured (rate card, tier, streak/team bonus, card credits, cap_event, lap_bonus_credits, createCompany) and, as a net, fails the row at send time without calling the provider; nothing is split. (2) No automatic top-up of a partial grant and no revive of failed rewards (FUTURE, reachable only under the test-only FUTURE_OPS switch; routes retry-partials and revive-failed answer 409 not_enabled_in_v1; a failed period row is not re-sent by the daily settle). A failed or partially granted reward needs an admin: overview shows it (open_failed_rewards, open_partial_grants), the admin checks the Moneypenny ledger, then POST /api/admin/reissue {id, confirm:true, amount?} closes the old row (failed -> void 'reissued'; partial keeps what was delivered, last_error 'closed_partial') and creates a NEW reward (new id = new reference, new idempotency key, caps skipped), audit line reward.reissued. Note: the stored state of such a row is 'failed' (or 'sent' with last_error partial_grant); the admin-facing name is needs-admin. (3) duplicate_ref on a send takes the amount only from the provider history (ref manual:<ref>, adapter reads e.ref ?? e.ext_ref); unconfirmed -> failed 'duplicate_unconfirmed', never paid again by itself. (4) An erased person's rows are closed for good (void, last_error voided_erased, audit line with maybe_landed) and skipped by settle, retry and the overview. (5) The clock drives order_release ops (a declined order's hold) until the provider confirms. Known and documented, not fixed: rotating the Moneypenny key invalidates the idempotency keys of calls whose reply was lost (a retry under the same key then gets idempotency_conflict, retriable forever): finish or check pending credits before rotating.
